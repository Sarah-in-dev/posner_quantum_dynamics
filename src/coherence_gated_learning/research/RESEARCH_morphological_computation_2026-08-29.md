## 1. MECHANISTIC INVARIANTS

Seven. I state each with the case that **breaks** it, because the breaks are more informative than the matches.

**I1 — Two rates, and the slow variable *parameterises* the fast one.**
Not merely "two timescales." In every biological case the slow variable determines the fast variable's *dynamical structure*, not just its initial condition:
- *Physarum*: fast = the Kirchhoff/Poiseuille solve (memoryless, re-solved every step by ICCG); slow = `dD/dt = f(|Q|) − D`. D is the resistance in the fast solve.
- Membrane: slow = lipid composition → sets T_c → sets susceptibility χ and correlation length ξ of the fast field φ(r). The slow memory is literally the *gain* of the fast inference.
- Bioelectric: slow = channel/GJ expression repertoire → determines **whether the fast system is bistable at all** (Law & Levin: Nav1.6 ≥10:1 over leak gives bistability in mammalian ionic conditions, not amphibian; Kv1.x expression destroys it).
- Cuttlefish: slow = motor-unit and hierarchy-node wiring → determines which components exist.

**Breaks:** open-loop reservoir computing (W_out is a record read off the fast state; it never re-enters the physics) and CGL (`store` is written by `reward()` and read by `value()`, but nothing in `store` ever touches `trace`, `bonded`, `w`, or η). These are exactly the two systems the literature refuses to call learners. That correspondence is not a coincidence — it is the diagnostic.

**I2 — One state variable per element is simultaneously the memory and the transfer coefficient.**
D_ij is the record of past flux *and* the present conductance. V_i is the decision variable *and* the thing that gates g_ij. φ(r) is the inference *and* the gate on every inclusion. There is no weights-vs-activations split anywhere in the biology. CGL breaks this by construction: `trace` (float, decays, wiped at commit) and `store` (dict, never decays) are different objects with different lifetimes and no coupling.

**I3 — The local rule is local; the *signal it consumes* is the solution of a globally constrained problem.**
This is the single most under-appreciated invariant and the Physarum finding states it as the top caveat. `dD/dt = f(|Q|) − D` is local, but Q is the unique unit flow minimising Σ R_e Q_e² under fixed I_0 (Thomson's principle) — every edge's flux already encodes the whole topology. Same shape elsewhere: Vmem is the solution of an electrodiffusion problem over the whole coupled tissue under charge conservation; the LC director field is the global minimum of Frank elasticity vs anchoring; near-critical membranes get long-range coupling from ξ. **The algorithm has no global signal; the substrate broadcasts one.**
**Breaks:** cuttlefish, provably — no skin-to-brain feedback pathway is known and the animal cannot see its own mantle. And CGL, which has no conserved quantity upstream of selection.

**I4 — Commitment is loss of stability of a fixed point, not the output of a comparator.**
`det J(B) = 1 − μ` (Physarum); extrapolation length ℓ = K/W crossing system size L with topological charge conservation forbidding intermediates (LC droplet); separatrix crossing with GJ self-isolation (bioelectric); P/L* first-order insertion transition (AMPs); digital ERK bistability with SHP-1 feedback (T cell). Self-timing, hysteretic, no arbitration step.
**Breaks:** cuttlefish (Chiao & Hanlon: "no clear threshold at which visual contrast suddenly induced all of the cuttlefish to produce the White square") and CGL (two independent Bernoulli coins against a threshold — the comparator form the biology avoids, with no hysteresis and no basin).

**I5 — Active on, passive off: a conditional growth term against an unconditional decay term.**
Chromatophore expansion is muscular, retraction is elastic recoil. D grows with f(|Q|), decays at fixed r. Traces decay to floor. Bonds dissolve at k_release. The asymmetry does real work: uniform decay against evidence-proportional growth **removes low-evidence elements first** (Kramar & Alim: idle networks prune thin tubes first, so memory-bearing thick tubes survive longest). Forgetting is signal-preserving for free.
**CGL has the decay but on the wrong variable:** bonds die (in `network_cgl`) at a *uniform* rate independent of evidence, and `store` never decays at all. The forgetting curve is inverted relative to biology.

**I6 — Dissipation is the licence, not the loss.** Contraction is what makes the state a well-defined function of input history (echo state property), pins the resting potential as a convergent attractor (BETSE reaches the Goldman-predicted V from grossly non-physiological initial conditions), and makes the answer reproducible and therefore usable. Every design change that reduces dissipation "to preserve information" trades away the property that makes the system computable at all.

**I7 (negative, and the strongest) — Not one of these systems computes a distance-to-target and attributes it backward.** No error signal, no gradient, no credit assignment, in any of the six. Every finding's disanalogy list says so independently. The apparent goal-directedness is relaxation to a fixed point; the apparent optimality is a theorem *about* the dynamics discovered afterwards (Thomson, Straszak & Vishnoi's mirror-descent result), not an objective the dynamics descend. **The only exception in the entire set is the immune system** — see §7, D7.

## 2. WHAT REPLACES THE MAP — SEVEN DISTINCT STRATEGIES, NOT ONE

They are not variants of one idea; they answer *different questions* and have different failure modes.

| Strategy | Mechanism | Question it answers | Hard cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M1 Physical instantiation of the hypothesis space** | Fill the maze; initialise a fine mesh spanning the arena. Every candidate physically exists before evaluation | "what are my options?" | f(0)=0 ⇒ monotone subtractive; cannot discover structure absent from initialisation; construction cost is the whole space |
| **M2 Boundary condition as the reference** | The currently adsorbed monolayer *is* the reference; complement's default-on C3b + factor-H tag | "does this belong?" | ~1 bit per droplet/domain; specificity must be imported from chemistry |
| **M3 Setpoint + distance-to-bifurcation** | Distance-to-T_c held by homeoviscous adaptation; T-cell threshold tuned to chronic ambient signal | "is this above background?" | O(1) storage but scalar verdict only; nobody knows how to train an *array* of these jointly |
| **M4 Self-referential scaling in the actuator basis** | "object area = 40–120% of *my* White square" — invariant over a 28-fold size range with zero feedback | "how big / what kind?" | Can only express what the ~30-entry dictionary expresses — deliberately |
| **M5 Local spatial-derivative comparison** | own V − mean(interior neighbours) > θ (Durant 2019) | "where am I?" | Origin-free and fragment-invariant, but demonstrated only for a *switch*, never a shape |
| **M6 Temporal→spatial unfolding** | Input history embedded in the instantaneous physical configuration; recover it with one linear measurement | "what just happened?" | Fading memory *forbids* accumulation — theorem-level, not an engineering gap |
| **M7 Absence coding / negative marking** | Holes in shape space after negative selection; missing-self | "what is anomalous?" | Coarse; generalises by physical similarity, hence off-target action |

Two of these (M2, M7) invert *where* storage lives: the tag is on the object, distributed, so cost scales with the number of objects, not the number of categories.

CGL has a weak M6 (the trace bridges delayed credit; horizon ≈ τ·ln((1−floor)/(thresh−floor)) ≈ 107) and nothing else. It has no map because it is never asked to navigate: `choose(candidates)` is handed the option set *and* each option's unit set every call. The caller holds the map and hands it back.

## 3. THE OUTPUT — AND WHAT "READING A STRUCTURE" ACTUALLY MEANS

Five output types across the set:

1. **Structure that IS the machine.** *Physarum*'s surviving subgraph is simultaneously "the shortest path" and the transport system that carries protoplasm. The membrane's domain pattern is read in parallel by every inclusion. **No decode step exists because none is needed.**
2. **Structure read by a third party.** The cuttlefish skin pattern is read by a predator's visual system. The return signal is binary, delayed by an unbounded interval, and never re-enters the circuit within an episode.
3. **Structure as a dynamical object.** Closed-loop morphological computation: the output is a limit cycle; "reading" it means letting the thing run. This is the *only* route in the RC paradigm to a persistent structure rather than a value stream.
4. **Value stream.** Open-loop RC: y(t) = W_out·x(t). The high-dimensional transient exists but is discarded through the projection.
5. **Action index / scalar.** CGL: `choose() → int`, `value() → float`.

Four operational criteria for "the output is a structure," which are what actually matter for the redesign:

- **Reading is using.** The test is not "does a graph exist internally" but "does the graph do work without being re-encoded." CGL fails this precisely: `components()` is called from exactly one site (`reward():240`) and its output is immediately reduced to `frozenset(members)` — a dict key. A path a–b–c and a triangle a–b–c produce the *identical* key. The topology is computed and discarded in the same expression.
- **Reading is plural and projection-dependent.** In the membrane there is no privileged reader; partitioning, tension, permeability and Casimir forces are four different functionals of one field. A single scalar readout collapses a structural output into a value output by definition.
- **The informative scalar is usually a functional of the *boundary*, not the bulk.** Papahadjopoulos: permeability peaks *at* the transition because the leak path is the domain perimeter. If a structure must be reduced to a number, measure interface length / cut size / boundary crossings — not phase occupancy. (`caps.append(len(L.group_w))` is a capacity count, not a correspondence measure.)
- **Structural outputs generally do not halt.** Mayne: *Physarum* is "always in a state of flux… user input is required to determine when the operation has finished." The exception is relaxation, where convergence *is* the halt signal — the one clean advantage of relaxation over filtering.

**The hardest finding in this section:** *no system in the set both produces a structure and scores it against an external objective.* Physarum's structure is optimal by a theorem about physics. The cuttlefish's is evaluated only outside the loop by a predator. The bioelectric structure is latched by chromatin, never scored. This is the same gap the CGL audit found ("NO code path anywhere scores the grown structure against the environment's structure") — and the biology does not supply the missing piece. The boundary-systems finding states it flatly: any transfer must supply the objective from outside; the physics will not.

## 4. MEMORY vs PROCESS

Three regimes, and the distinction is sharper than "memory = process":

- **R1 Identity.** Memory *is* the transfer coefficient: D_ij, φ(r), the LC boundary condition, the reservoir transient, the cuttlefish motor plant's covariance (Reiter recovered the hierarchy from co-fluctuation statistics alone — only possible because the memory *is* the covariance).
- **R2 Two registers, bidirectionally coupled.** Fast state ⇄ slow landscape: Vmem ⇄ channel/GJ expression, with a real molecular return path (voltage-dependent butyrate transport via SLC5A8 → HDAC inhibition → chromatin). Membrane φ ⇄ lipid composition. Immune signalling ⇄ tuned threshold. **This is the only architecture in the set that is a candidate learning primitive**, because the fast variable edits the parameters that define its own attractors.
- **R3 Split, one-way.** State → record, no back-coupling: reservoir x → W_out; CGL trace/bonded → store. Fixed feature expansion plus an external head.

What follows from the absence of a model/inference split:

(a) **Capacity is hard-capped and cannot be grown by running longer.** Dambre: total capacity ≤ number of *linearly independent observed* state variables. An "infinite-dimensional" soft body with 10 sensors has capacity 10.
(b) **Recall is relaxation, so the stopping criterion is free.** R3 systems have no stopping criterion at all.
(c) **Forgetting is ordered and free** (I5). A separate store cannot do this without an explicit schedule.
(d) **Memories superpose on a shared substrate rather than overwriting.** Kramar & Alim: new stimuli "differentially reinforce and weaken existing thick tubes in superposition of existing memories." A dict of frozensets cannot superpose — the keys are disjoint, and a novel set falls through to a Jaccard blur (measured cost: 23714 vs 2581 control).
(e) **Executing the output destroys the substrate that held it.** CGL's `reward()` sets `trace[m] = trace_floor`; bioelectric commitment physically rebuilds the tissue. No epoch, no batch, no rollback, no replay.
(f) **Surviving substrate turnover requires the code to live in the wiring/covariance, not in the tokens.** The cuttlefish demonstrates this (new chromatophores inserted into existing components at a rate matched to recolouring; pattern statistics conserved across complete hardware replacement). The planarian case is the warning: a two-headed phenotype persists through months of full cell turnover and **the slow register has never been identified** — Durant et al. excluded histology, markers and stem-cell distribution, but *not* chromatin or small RNAs.

## 5. SELECTION

| System | Mechanism | Is it competitive? |
|---|---|---|
| *Physarum* | (i) f-independent exponential death where topology forces Q≡0; (ii) bifurcation at det J = 1−μ under fixed I_0 | (ii) yes — but competition is *induced by the conserved budget*, not implemented by a comparator |
| Cuttlefish | Trigger feature crosses a tuning band → hierarchy node recruits 1–11 components; contrast sets graded gain | **No.** No WTA, no mutual inhibition, no pruning, no phase transition |
| LC / membrane | Relaxation to a free-energy minimum in a topologically constrained space | Competition between two *energy terms*, never between candidate hypotheses |
| Immune | Kinetic proofreading (sharpness exponential in N, paid in ~50 s latency + dissipation); bistable ERK; literal clonal deletion; condensation-by-exclusion; autocatalysis arrested by tag | Yes, and it includes genuine pruning |
| Bioelectric | Thresholded local comparison; noise at a separatrix; WTA by GJ self-isolation; Notum⊣Wnt double-negative latch | Yes — but by self-isolation, not by mutual inhibition |
| Reservoir | None at runtime. One offline convex least-squares solve | No |
| CGL | Two independent Bernoulli coins, neither normalised across alternatives | No — and measured inadequate: P(pick best) = 0.384 at values 0.5/0.45/0.4 vs 0.333 uniform, *with perfect values supplied* |

**Common form: not competition — loss of stability under a slowly varying control parameter.** In four of six the discrete outcome appears because a fixed point loses stability or a separatrix is crossed, with the control parameter being reinforcement convexity μ, extrapolation length K/W vs L, gap-junction conductance, or feedback gain. Where competition exists it is *induced by a conserved quantity*, and where no conserved quantity exists (cuttlefish, RC) there is no selection mechanism at all — only recruitment and regression respectively.

Two corollaries with direct design bite:
- **The scarce resource must be spent BEFORE the outcome, not after.** *Physarum*'s I_0 is fixed upstream of the flux computation, which is exactly why it selects. CGL consumes trace *after* committing, so scarcity cannot influence the selection it is supposed to drive.
- **A bifurcation is self-timing and hysteretic; a coin is neither.** Hysteresis is what makes commitment stick without a latch — and it is also what obstructs credit assignment. You cannot have both from the same mechanism.

## 6. PREVENT-UP-FRONT vs OVER-CONNECT-THEN-PRUNE

**Biology uses both, simultaneously, but on different variables. They are not alternatives; they occupy different layers.**

**Where (b) over-connect-then-prune actually appears:**
- *Physarum*, canonically: the maze is filled / a fine random mesh spans the arena, then `dD/dt = f(|Q|) − D`. **But pruning is scarcity-gated, not intrinsic** — Mayne: shortest-path-like graphs appear only in nutrient-limited environments; in nutrient-rich ones it stays over-connected, nearer a Gabriel graph.
- Immune repertoire: generate a huge random detector set, delete the ~5% that bind self. Pruned by a *signal* (dwell time), not by flux.
- Complement: indiscriminate autocatalytic C3b deposition on every surface, arrested locally by factor H.

**Where (a) prevent appears:**
- Cuttlefish, in the hardest possible form: motor units are small (90% innervate <14 chromatophores), only 3 pigments, "only two or perhaps three mottle sizes," a ~30-entry dictionary, and — the sharpest move — **the control grid is deliberately misaligned from the physical grid**, so per-pixel optimisation is not inefficient, it is *not expressible*. "Pixel" is not a type the system can name.
- Membrane sorting by physical fit (TMD length vs bilayer thickness 4.0/4.6 nm). LC: the sensor is *at* the defect — locality is total.
- Bioelectric GJ is a genuine hybrid and worth separating out: coupling is dense by default and the network **self-partitions by closing junctions where states disagree**. Over-connection is *dissolved by a state-dependent constraint*, continuously — neither pre-imposed capacity nor reward-driven pruning.

**The determination is mechanistic, not aesthetic: (b) is licensed exactly where a conserved global signal exists.** Cuttlefish is a pure (a) system *because it has no feedback channel at all* — you cannot prune what you cannot measure. *Physarum* is a pure (b) system *because the flow is conserved*, which is what makes each edge's local flux globally informative.

**And the two are coupled at the mechanism level.** Tero's saturating f — `f(|Q|) = |Q|^γ/(1+|Q|^γ)` — **is a per-element capacity limit**, and it is what produces the saddle-node bifurcation creating a stable coexistence equilibrium. Unbounded reinforcement collapses to a tree; bounded reinforcement retains loops and hence fault tolerance (Tokyo comparison: FT 14±4% vs rail's 4%, at the same benefit/cost ratio). So: **a capacity limit is a component of the pruning dynamics, not an alternative to it.** Correspondingly, μ>1 (aggressive pruning) *narrows the basin of attraction to the shortest path* — speed trades directly against correctness, and fast pruning reliably locks in the wrong answer.

**Buys / costs:**

*(a) buys*: bounded cost, no wasted construction, bad solutions made **inexpressible** rather than merely discouraged, robustness under substrate turnover, and it works with **no feedback channel whatsoever**.
*(a) costs*: the basis must be right a priori; nothing outside it is discoverable; an outer loop (evolution/design) is required to set it; and **a wrong basis fails silently** — you get a plausible-looking output with no error signal. The colourblind cuttlefish successfully produces a variable it provably cannot measure, and that only works because evolution selected the primitives on that variable.

*(b) buys*: no need to know the answer's structure in advance; global optimality for free where the substrate supplies a conserved flow; graded-then-discrete output with no separate discretisation step; free structural pruning where the signal is structurally zero (dead ends die exponentially, f-independently).
*(b) costs*: you must physically build the whole hypothesis space; f(0)=0 makes it monotone subtractive (no regrowth — real regrowth is the body extending new tubes, *outside the model*); path-dependence when pruning is fast; and it is void without a globally constrained signal.

**Where CGL actually sits — and this is the crux for the redesign.** It has (a) without (b), and its (a) is unearned:
- `cgl_primitive.activate()` bonds *every* pair among active ∪ recent, unconditionally — no distance, no capacity. Bonds are boolean and are never dissolved within an episode; they are wiped wholesale at `clear_episode()`. Edges "vanish" only because `fidelity() = outer(trace,trace)·bonded` is thresholded at 0.5, i.e. by **recency**, never by evidence.
- `network_cgl.py` adds real (a) — `w = exp(−d/λ)`, a hard `spin_capacity` refusal counted as `n_frustrated`, and the η gate — plus dissolution at a **uniform** `k_release`. Uniform decay with no reinforcement arm is not (b); it is noise.
- **No edge anywhere carries a scalar that reward modifies.** Reinforcement acts only on `store` (frozenset→float). The learning signal never touches the structure.
- And the (a) parameters (`coupling_length`, `spin_capacity`, η, the 117-value-unit vs 22-slot encoding) are *handed in by the caller*, not selected against the objective. So CGL pays (a)'s cost — nothing discoverable outside the basis — without (a)'s benefit, an evolutionarily-vetted basis. RESULT_network_primitive states the consequence: "the network binds by CO-OCCURRENCE IN THE INPUT, which is unrelated to PREDICTIVENESS OF REWARD… the single most predictive feature is absent from ~90% of the components formed."

**The minimal repair implied by the invariants is one of exactly two, and they are mutually exclusive:**
1. **Go (b) properly.** Give edges a graded conductance D_ij; add a conserved budget upstream of the signal (so competition is induced, not asserted); make reward drive `dD/dt = f(flux) − γD` with **saturating** f (to buy redundancy and avoid tree collapse); expose convexity μ as the single explore/exploit dial (det J = 1−μ) instead of a coin. This requires solving the hard part first: what is the conserved, globally-constrained flux? Without it there are no guarantees, only a plausible-looking local rule.
2. **Go (a) properly.** Keep the capacity/locality constraints, drop the pretence of structure discovery, and move the basis selection to an outer loop scored on the objective — the cuttlefish arrangement. This is honest and cheap and needs no feedback channel, but then the system does not learn structure and should not claim to.

What is *not* available is the current position: capacity constraints with a caller-supplied basis, plus uniform random dissolution, plus a reward signal that only ever touches a lookup table.

## 7. WHERE THESE SYSTEMS GENUINELY DISAGREE

**D1 — Is there a conserved global signal?** *Physarum*, bioelectric, membrane/LC: yes (current, charge, free energy). Cuttlefish: **no, provably** — no known skin-to-brain feedback, and the animal cannot see its own dorsal mantle. This single fact propagates into every downstream difference.

**D2 — Bifurcation or recruitment?** *Physarum*/LC/bioelectric/immune commit by bifurcation: hysteretic, self-timing, no comparator. The cuttlefish does **not** — Osorio et al. 2022 ran the direct test of the winner-take-all body-pattern model and it largely failed (PC1 = 32%, PC2 = 16.2%, no scree elbow, patterns "scattered across this plane, with little evidence for clumping"; components expressible independently). Whether a component fires is categorical and saturating (4 white squares out of 320 = 1.25% suffices); how hard it fires is graded and **thresholdless**. Do not average this with the bifurcation systems — the field's own best test says the flagship "commitment" system does not commit that way.

**D3 — What is the yardstick?** Three incompatible answers. Cuttlefish: the animal's own output primitives (40–120% of its own White square, invariant over 28×). *Physarum*/bioelectric: no units at all — the reference is a conservation law and a fixed point. Membrane/LC: a setpoint in an extrinsic physical variable (T_c, anchoring energy W).

**D4 — Must the state converge?** Relaxation systems (LC, membrane, bioelectric, Hopfield, *Physarum*) **must** converge; the fixed point is the answer. Reservoir computing **forbids** convergence — Nakajima frames RC explicitly as escaping the convergence time of attractor networks; transients are the product. Cuttlefish contains *both regimes in one actuator*: blanching is fast, low-dimensional, open-loop, ballistic, converging to the same endpoint from different starts; camouflage is high-dimensional, waypointed, degenerate, feedback-dependent, 40–126 s. **Regime, not architecture, decides whether behaviour looks like commitment or like search.** You cannot get relaxation's structured output and filtering's real-time latency from one operating point.

**D5 — Who reads the output?** Nobody (Physarum — reading is using), a predator (cuttlefish), every inclusion in parallel (membrane), an external experimenter (RC, and — per Horsman — *necessarily*: "if there is a computer, what is using it?").

**D6 — Does memory survive substrate turnover?** Cuttlefish: yes, demonstrated, by matching insertion rate to recolouring rate. Planaria: yes phenotypically, mechanism unidentified. RC: no — W_out is fitted to one material at one temperature with one sensor calibration.

**D7 — Does anything learn within a lifetime?** Honest count: **one.** Cuttlefish is explicitly hardwired (Barbosa: "a single visual sampling rule that scales accurately during ontogeny," achieved "without visual confirmation"). *Physarum* adapts within an episode but the optimisation is a theorem about the physics, not an objective it descends. RC's substrate never adapts. Bioelectric has the fast⇄slow return path mechanistically but no demonstrated within-lifetime shaping toward better morphogenetic outcomes; the landscape is evolved, and the one paper that trains a bioelectric network does so *in silico with backprop and an external optimiser*.

**The exception is the immune system, and its profile is instructive:** it is the only within-lifetime learner in the set, and it is also the only one that (i) over-produces a random population and prunes it by a signal, (ii) has an explicit and brutally asymmetric loss function, (iii) sharpens discrimination by kinetic proofreading — buying exponential sharpness in N at the price of ~50 s latency and dissipation, (iv) tunes a threshold to the chronic ambient background so the decision runs on ligand *quality* rather than quantity, and (v) **keeps a partial explicit catalogue anyway** (AIRE-driven promiscuous expression of thousands of tissue-restricted antigens). Not one of the elegant map-free physics systems learns. If the redesign wants learning, the immune profile — not the *Physarum* profile — is the precedent, and it comes with a catalogue, an objective and a latency bill attached.

**D8 — They disagree on whether this is computation at all.** Müller & Hoffmann rule the passive dynamic walker "pure physics walking" and accept only reservoir computing. Horsman's criterion (tested theory, fixed encode/decode pair, a fundamental physical operation, an ε-commuting diagram, **abstract description available a priori**) cuts straight through this set: *Physarum*'s shortest path passes (the problem is specified in advance and the decoder is fixed — support of D), while bioelectric target morphology fails (no decoder exists; the mapping is only ever validated forward, and Levin's own whitepaper states the representation problem as an open goal). The default failure mode is picking the representation after seeing the result — "the soap bubble has solved a complex optimization problem." For the redesign this is a lab-discipline requirement, not philosophy: **write down the decoder and the predicted abstract trajectory before the run**, or the structural claim will be unfalsifiable in exactly the documented way.

## 1. What this primitive is FOR

**Restatement.** CGL is not a reinforcement learner and should stop pretending to be one. It is a candidate **online structure-discovery primitive for the data-poor, transitively-constrained regime**: a device that consumes a stream of undifferentiated unit-activation events and emits a *carving* — which units belong together — under conditions where the carving is never directly observed and there are far fewer events than there are pairs to count.

Specifically, it is the right tool for problems with all four of:

1. **No given carving.** The input is a stream of unit ids with no feature boundaries, no slots, no schema. (The mushroom benchmark violates this fatally: 117 feature-values is a carving handed over pre-made. Synthesis §6: "the caller holds the map and hands it back.")
2. **The informative relation is never observed directly.** Group membership must be inferred transitively — you see (a,b) and (b,c), never (a,c), and must nonetheless place a, b, c together. This is invariant I3 in its operational form: *the local signal must be the solution of a globally constrained problem*, or there is nothing for the substrate to add over a counter.
3. **Events ≪ pairs.** The regime where a co-occurrence matrix is mostly zeros. Above that threshold, counting is near-optimal and this primitive has no reason to exist.
4. **The deliverable is the structure itself,** scored against a generative partition by a decoder fixed in advance.

**Explicit anti-scope, and it is not a matter of taste.** CGL is the wrong tool for action selection among close-valued alternatives. `RESULT_nonstationary_bandit.md` measured the mechanical ceiling: P(best) = 0.384 versus 0.333 uniform *with perfect values supplied*. That is not a tuning failure; independent unnormalised Bernoulli coins cannot rank. Synthesis §5 names the same thing structurally — no system in the biological set selects by a comparator; where selection exists it is a bifurcation induced by a conserved budget. `choose()` should be deleted, not fixed.

**Falsifier for this whole framing.** If a leaky co-occurrence counter with one round of transitive smoothing matches CGL at recovering generative groups in the data-poor regime (§6's C2 control), then there is no problem class left for this primitive and it should be retired. I consider this a live possibility, maybe 40%.

The measured cost is on record: Jaccard partial reactivation 23714 vs 2581 control. A wholesale wipe is an amnesia schedule bolted on where the dynamics should have produced the ordering. It also makes the "episode" a caller-supplied boundary — one more piece of the map handed in.

**R4. Boolean `bonded`.** Replace with graded `D` (below). This is the single highest-leverage change: with a boolean matrix there is *no variable that reward or evidence could modify even in principle*, which is why every learning signal in the codebase had to be routed into a side dict.

**R5. The hard spin-ledger refusal** *(network_cgl.py:87-88, 110-112)*.

Synthesis §6, and this is the sharpest technical point in the whole document: **Tero's saturating f — `f(Q) = Q^μ/(1+Q^μ)` — IS a per-element capacity limit**, and it is what produces the saddle-node bifurcation giving a stable coexistence equilibrium (unbounded reinforcement collapses to a tree; bounded reinforcement retains loops, hence fault tolerance). A capacity limit is a *component of the pruning dynamics*, not an alternative to it. The current code has the capacity limit as an order-dependent yes/no refusal — the `rng.shuffle(pairs)` at :104 exists precisely to hide that order-dependence — and consequently `n_frustrated` is a diagnostic counter with no dynamical consequence and no consumer.

**R6. Assigned random `positions`** *(network_cgl.py:71-72)*.
The docstring is honest that this is a modelling choice. The synthesis makes it a fatal one: §6, "(a) costs: a wrong basis fails silently." A random 2-D embedding is an unvetted basis, and CGL pays (a)'s full cost — nothing outside the basis is discoverable — while getting none of (a)'s benefit. Delete the geometry. ### ADD / CHANGE

**A1. Graded conductance `D_ij` — memory and transfer coefficient in one object.**
*Grounding:* I2 (D_ij is the record of past flux and the present conductance; V_mem is the decision variable and the gate; φ(r) is the inference and the gate). *Computationally:* gives reinforcement something on the structure to act on, makes superposition possible (§4d: "new stimuli differentially reinforce and weaken existing thick tubes in superposition" — a dict of disjoint frozensets provably cannot do this), and makes the readout thresholdable rather than categorical. *Differs from now:* `bonded` is boolean and `store` is elsewhere.

*Model 6 reading:* D_ij is the maintained entangled-dimer density on a link — a continuous quantity, more natural to Model 6 than the boolean already is.

**A2. The conserved global signal — a Kirchhoff flow solve. This is the load-bearing addition and the hard part.**
*Grounding:* I3, stated by the synthesis as the most under-appreciated invariant: `dD/dt = f(|Q|) − D` is local, but Q is the unique flow minimising Σ R_e Q_e² under fixed I_0 (Thomson), so every edge's flux already encodes the whole topology. §6: **"(b) is licensed exactly where a conserved global signal exists."** Without this, adding graded weights and a decay term produces a plausible-looking local rule with no guarantees — which is what CGL already is.

*Concretely.* At event t with active set A_t, treat every ordered pair (s,u) ∈ A_t × A_t as a unit source/sink and compute edge flows on the current conductance graph. One pseudo-inverse gives all of them analytically:

```
L   = diag(D.sum(1)) - D                     # weighted Laplacian
Lp  = pinv(L)                                # one solve per event (or per k events)
Q_ij(s,u) = D_ij * (Lp[i,s] - Lp[i,u] - Lp[j,s] + Lp[j,u])
Q_ij      = sum over pairs (s,u) in A_t of |Q_ij(s,u)|
```

Cost at n=120: pinv ≈ 1.7e6 flops, sub-millisecond; refresh every k=10 events since D moves slowly.

*What it buys that counting cannot:* transitive evidence. Seeing (a,b) then (b,c) raises the flow on the a–c route without (a,c) ever occurring. The current Jaccard blur is a crude, measurably bad 1-hop version of this.

*Uncertainty, stated plainly and at the same honesty level as network_cgl's own `positions` docstring:* **the source/sink convention is a modelling choice, not something the biology dictates.** All-pairs-within-the-active-set is the standard Physarum multi-source formulation, but "the active set is the source set" is an assumption. Flag it in the docstring; it is the single most attackable assumption in the redesign.

*And a real opportunity:* Model 6 already has a genuine conservation law — **the 31P spin budget**. Four spins per dimer, spent by intra- and cross-synapse bonds alike. §5's corollary: "the scarce resource must be spent BEFORE the outcome." The spin ledger is a conserved quantity implemented in the wrong form — as a post-hoc comparator instead of as a budget that shapes the flow upstream. I have not verified whether the Model 6 spin accounting supports a genuine flow formulation; that is the highest-value thing to check next, because if it does, the conserved signal is *derived* rather than imported from slime mould.

**A3. The one update rule: conditional growth against unconditional decay, with saturating f.**
```
dD_ij/dt = f(s_ij) - gamma * D_ij ,    f(x) = x**mu / (1 + x**mu)
```
with `s_ij` the salience of §5. *Grounding:* I5 (active on / passive off) and §6's coupling of capacity to pruning. *What it does:* f's saturation is the per-element capacity (replacing R5), gamma is the old `k_release` now doing real work, and the asymmetry makes forgetting signal-preserving for free. `f(0)=0` makes the process monotone-subtractive within a run — an accepted cost, and the honest note that real regrowth in *Physarum* happens outside the model (the body extends new tubes).

**A4. μ as the single explore/exploit dial, replacing every coin.**
*Grounding:* I4 — commitment is loss of stability of a fixed point, not the output of a comparator; `det J(B) = 1 − μ`. *What it does:* μ < 1 → coexistence equilibrium, loops retained, broad basin; μ > 1 → competitive exclusion toward a tree. Self-timing and hysteretic; no epsilon, no temperature, no `p_commit`, no `rng.random() < p`. *Differs from now:* the two coins at `cgl_primitive.py:242` and `:211` both disappear.

*Pre-registered cost, from §6:* μ > 1 **narrows the basin of attraction** — fast pruning reliably locks in the wrong answer. Speed trades directly against correctness. This gives a falsifiable prediction (§6 ablation).

**A5. A second, slow register with a real return path.**
*Grounding:* §4-R2, the only architecture in the set that is a candidate learning primitive, "because the fast variable edits the parameters that define its own attractors." Bioelectric has the molecular return path (V-dependent butyrate transport → HDAC inhibition → chromatin); membranes have lipid composition setting T_c and hence the susceptibility of the fast field.
*Concretely:* a per-edge chronic baseline `Qbar_ij` on a slow timescale, which sets the salience threshold that gates the fast update (§5). This satisfies I1 properly: the slow variable **parameterises** the fast one's gain rather than merely initialising it. This is one line of code and it is what separates a learner from a filter.

**A6. Readout by effective resistance, not by key lookup.**
`R_eff(i,j) = Lp[i,i] + Lp[j,j] - 2*Lp[i,j]`, free from the pseudo-inverse you already computed. No store, no key, no Jaccard. §3's criterion: *reading is using* — the same object that carries the flow answers the query.

### What survives from the current build

Three of the seven listed mechanisms survive and one survives transformed:
- **Two-timescale binding** (fast formation `bind_window=2.0`, long persistence `tau=216`) — survives intact, and gains a principled reading as I1's fast/slow split.
- **Trace decay with a floor** — survives as the fast variable feeding the flow solve.
- **Readout consumes its substrate** (`trace[m] = trace_floor`, :247) — survives; §4e, and it is the mechanism that makes credit self-limiting.
- **Conjunctive credit via a shared coin** — survives *only as an idea*, re-implemented as a bifurcation rather than a coin. The insight (credit attaches to a group, which a scalar eligibility trace cannot express) is correct and is the best thing in the current build. The coin is the wrong vehicle for it.

## 3. Should the output be the grown structure?

**Yes, and this is not optional under the reframing** — but the synthesis also supplies the hard constraint that makes the naive version unfalsifiable, and it must be respected.

**The hard constraint first.** §3: *no system in the entire set both produces a structure and scores it against an external objective.* Physarum's structure is optimal by a theorem about physics, not an objective it descends. The cuttlefish's is evaluated only outside the loop, by a predator, on an unbounded delay. The bioelectric structure is latched by chromatin and never scored. The boundary-systems finding says it flatly: **the physics will not supply the objective; it must be imported.** So "make the output the structure" does not eliminate the scoring problem — it relocates it to the experimenter, where it must be handled with Horsman discipline: **write down the decoder and the predicted abstract trajectory before the run.** §8's named default failure mode is picking the representation after seeing the result.

**What concretely is read out.** The conductance matrix `D`, and two fixed functionals of it:

1. **The partition.** Decoder, fixed in advance: for each unit i, rank all others by `R_eff(i,·)` ascending and take the top k−1, where k is the true group size (supplied identically to every arm — a map handed in, but handed in symmetrically, which is what makes the comparison legitimate). No threshold, hence no free parameter to tune on the answer.
2. **The boundary functional.** §3: *the informative scalar is usually a functional of the boundary, not the bulk* — Papahadjopoulos, permeability peaks at the transition because the leak path is the domain perimeter. So the second score is the **cut ratio**: Σ D over within-group pairs divided by Σ D over across-group pairs, evaluated on the *true* partition using the *learned* D. This is strictly more informative than a partition score because it degrades gracefully — it detects a half-formed carving that a hard partition metric reports as failure. `caps.append(len(L.group_w))` in `benchmark_scaling.py` is the anti-pattern: a capacity count, not a correspondence measure.

**And the criterion that actually matters — reading is using.** §3's operational test is not "does a graph exist internally" but "does the graph do work without being re-encoded." The current code fails this in a single expression (`components()` → `frozenset` at :240-243). The redesign passes it structurally: `D` is simultaneously the memory, the transfer coefficient in the flow solve, and the thing `R_eff` is read from. There is no decode step because none is needed.

**One thing this costs you.** §3: structural outputs generally do not halt (Mayne on *Physarum*: "always in a state of flux… user input is required to determine when the operation has finished"). The exception is relaxation, where convergence *is* the halt signal — and A3's dynamics are relaxation, so you get the stopping criterion free. That is the one clean advantage over the current build, which has no stopping criterion at all.

## 4. Capacity-up-front (a) versus over-connect-then-prune (b)

**(b), and it is not close — but with a precondition that must be met first or (b) is void.**

**Why the current position is untenable rather than merely suboptimal.** §6 is unambiguous that CGL has (a) without (b), and that its (a) is unearned:
- (a)'s benefit is that bad solutions become **inexpressible** rather than discouraged, and the parade case (cuttlefish: motor units of <14 chromatophores, 3 pigments, a ~30-entry dictionary, control grid deliberately misaligned from the physical grid so "pixel" is not a type the system can name) works *only* because an outer loop — evolution — selected those primitives against the objective over evolutionary time.
- CGL's (a) parameters (`coupling_length`, `spin_capacity`, `eta`, the 117-value-vs-22-slot encoding) are handed in by the caller and never selected against anything. `RESULT_network_primitive.md` states the consequence exactly: the network binds by co-occurrence in the input, which is unrelated to predictiveness of reward, and the single most predictive feature is absent from ~90% of components formed.
- So CGL pays (a)'s full cost — nothing outside the basis is discoverable, and a wrong basis fails silently — for none of (a)'s benefit.

**The mechanistic licensing condition, which decides it.** §6: **"(b) is licensed exactly where a conserved global signal exists."** *Physarum* is a pure (b) system *because* the flow is conserved, which is what makes each edge's local flux globally informative. The cuttlefish is a pure (a) system *because it has no feedback channel at all* — you cannot prune what you cannot measure (D1: no known skin-to-brain pathway, and the animal cannot see its own dorsal mantle).

CGL has an unexploited conservation law sitting in it (the spin budget) and no feedback channel that reaches the structure. So the choice is: build the flow (A2) and go (b) properly, or admit there is no global signal and go (a) properly — meaning drop the claim of structure discovery entirely and move basis selection to an outer loop scored on the objective. §6 gives exactly these two and says they are mutually exclusive.

**I recommend (b), because (a)-properly is a different project.** (a)-properly is honest, cheap, needs no feedback, and would probably work — but it is a hyperparameter search over encoders, and it explicitly does not learn structure. The thing that makes this program interesting is the discovery claim.

**Three qualifications, all from the synthesis, that keep (b) from being naive.**

1. **Pruning must be scarcity-gated.** Mayne: shortest-path-like graphs appear only in nutrient-limited environments; in nutrient-rich ones *Physarum* stays over-connected, near a Gabriel graph. So the total injected current `I_0` and the decay rate `gamma` jointly set the scarcity, and they are the parameters that decide whether you get a carving or a blob. Fix `I_0` per event, upstream of the flux computation — §5's corollary: **the scarce resource must be spent BEFORE the outcome, not after.** CGL currently consumes trace *after* committing (:247), so scarcity cannot influence the selection it is meant to drive.
2. **Keep the capacity limit — as the saturation of f, not as a refusal** (R5/A3). They are not alternatives; unbounded reinforcement collapses to a tree, and bounded reinforcement is what retains loops and fault tolerance (Tokyo rail comparison, FT 14±4% vs 4%; unverified against source).
3. **Bioelectric gap junctions are the model for the hybrid,** and worth stealing if the pure-(b) version is unstable: coupling is dense by default, and the network **self-partitions by closing junctions where states disagree** — over-connection dissolved by a state-dependent constraint, continuously. That is a strictly better fallback than a fixed degree cap, and it is a two-line change to the decay term (`gamma_ij = gamma_0 * (1 + kappa*|trace_i - trace_j|)`). Listed as a contingency, not part of the primary design.

## 5. "Interesting" / salience — the actual update rule

**What the synthesis rules out, first, because this is where the intuition usually goes wrong.**

- **Prediction error, in the RL sense: ruled out.** Invariant I7 is the strongest finding in the set: not one of these systems computes a distance-to-target and attributes it backward. No error signal, no gradient, no credit assignment, in any of the six. Apparent goal-directedness is relaxation to a fixed point; apparent optimality is a theorem discovered afterwards.
- **Free energy, in the active-inference sense: ruled out.** Nothing in the set minimises a free energy over a *model*. The membrane and LC systems minimise a physical free energy over a *field*, which is relaxation, not inference — a different thing wearing the same word.
- **Novelty by count: ruled out empirically by your own code.** `cgl_primitive.py`'s docstring, mechanism 7: "a novelty-by-count drive was tried FIRST and failed."

**What the synthesis does support, and it supports exactly one thing:** strategy **M3 — setpoint plus distance-to-bifurcation** — in the form the immune system uses it. §7's D7 is decisive here: the immune system is the *only* within-lifetime learner in the entire set, and one of its five defining features is that it **tunes a threshold to the chronic ambient background so the decision runs on ligand *quality* rather than quantity.** Homeoviscous adaptation does the identical thing on the membrane: it holds *distance-to-T_c* constant, not temperature. Salience is deviation from an adapted baseline, and the baseline is a slow variable that the fast variable writes.

That is also precisely the human's intuition — move, encounter something above background, commit it — and it is the one form of "surprise" that requires no forward model.

### The rule

Per event t with active set A_t:

```python
# 1. fast: flow on the current structure (A2). I0 fixed upstream.
Q = flows(D, A_t)                                  # |Q_ij|, conserved by construction

# 2. slow register (A5): chronic ambient flux, alpha = 1/T_slow, T_slow >> tau
Qbar += alpha * (Q - Qbar)
Qvar += alpha * ((Q - Qbar)**2 - Qvar)

# 3. salience: flux above chronic background, in units of its own variability
s = np.maximum(0.0, (Q - Qbar) / (np.sqrt(Qvar) + eps))

# 4. the single update: conditional growth, unconditional decay (A3)
D += dt * ( s**mu / (1.0 + s**mu) - gamma * D )
D = np.maximum(D, D_floor)                          # a floor, not a wipe (replaces clear_episode)
```

Four properties worth naming:

- **`Q` is global, `s` is local.** I3 satisfied: the algorithm has no global signal; the substrate broadcasts one.
- **Normalising by `sqrt(Qvar)` is the quality-not-quantity move.** An edge that always carries high flux is not interesting; an edge that carries high flux *for it* is. This is what stops the hub units from swallowing everything, which is the failure mode I would otherwise expect first.
- **No reward appears.** Deliberate. If reward exists, it enters as a gain on the *same* variable — `D += dt*((1 + beta*(r - rbar)) * f(s) - gamma*D)`, with `rbar` a slow baseline (tonic-dopamine-as-average-rate; the synthesis flags this as UNVERIFIED and notes it made ENV-A worse at 1658.2 in the old framing, so treat `beta > 0` as an ablation, not a default). It never gets its own store. That is I1 and I2 held simultaneously, and it is the specific thing the current build violates.
- **Commitment is not in this rule.** There is no commit step, because §I4 says commitment is loss of stability, and A4 supplies it: as `D` grows on reinforced edges, the fixed-point structure of the same equation changes and the carving crystallises. Whether that actually happens at μ ≈ 1 with these dynamics is a claim I have not verified; it is Tero's result transplanted, and §6 is my only source for it.

**Optional second tier, which I am not confident earns its place.** A global salience gate: total dissipation `P = Σ D_ij Q_ij²` is free from the solve, and `|P − Pbar| > kappa*sigma_P` gates whether the event updates `D` at all. This is "the network's own energy landscape was surprised" and it is the closest thing to an event-level *interestingness*. It is cheap and gives a clean ablation, but I have no biological case in the supplied set that specifically motivates a dissipation-deviation gate, and it adds two parameters. Build it behind a flag; test it only if the primary rule works.

## 6. The decisive experiment

**Name:** *the room without a map.* No reward channel, no given carving, no episode boundaries. Minutes on a laptop. Designed to return a null and to have the null be informative.

### Generator

- 120 units: 8 latent groups × 8 members (64 signal units) + 56 pure distractors.
- Each event: draw a group g uniformly, emit **exactly one random pair from g** (never more), plus 1 uniformly-random distractor unit. Emit nothing else.
- Why one pair: each group has C(8,2)=28 internal pairs, 224 signal pairs total. At E=200 events, most signal pairs have been observed **zero times**. The group can only be recovered by transitive integration over paths. This is I3 turned into a task, and it is the only regime where a flow solve can beat a counter.
- Sweep E ∈ {100, 200, 400, 800, 1600, 3200}. 20 seeds.

### Decoder — fixed before the run, identical for every arm

For each signal unit i, rank all other units by the arm's similarity measure and take the top 7. **Score = precision@7** against true group membership, averaged over the 64 signal units. Distractor units scored separately as a contamination rate. Group size 7 is handed to every arm equally; there is no threshold anywhere, hence no parameter that can be tuned on the answer.

- **Flow arm** similarity: `-R_eff(i,j)` from the learned `D`.
- Count-based arms: the count/smoothed-count matrix directly.

Chance = 7/119 ≈ 0.059.

### Arms

| | |
|---|---|
| **TEST** | Redesigned primitive: graded `D`, flow solve (A2), salience rule (§5), μ = 0.8, no reward |
| **C1** | Leaky pair counts (`N *= exp(-dt/tau)`; `N[i,j] += 1` on co-occurrence), same decoder |
| **C2** | Leaky counts + one round of transitive smoothing: `S = N + lam * N@N / norm` |
| **C3** | Current CGL as built — `cgl_primitive` graph mode, `activate`/`decay`/`components`, similarity = Jaccard over `store` keys |
| **C4** | Shuffle control: group labels permuted per event. Marginals preserved, structure destroyed |

**C2 is the control that matters.** It is the cheap, non-physics way to get transitive evidence. If C2 matches TEST, the win was transitivity and *not* the conserved global signal — the honest conclusion would be "delete the flow solve, ship matrix squaring," and the specific claim this redesign rests on is dead even though the reframing survives.

### Pre-registered predictions

1. **Data-rich (E = 3200):** TEST ≈ C1 ≈ C2, all high. **Predicted, and explicitly NOT evidence for the reframing.** Stated in advance so it cannot be reported as a win.
2. **Data-poor (E ∈ {100, 200, 400}):** TEST ≥ C1 + 0.15 absolute, and TEST ≥ C2 + 0.05, at ≥2 of the 3 budgets, paired across 20 seeds, Wilcoxon p < 0.01.
3. **C3 < 0.25 at every E.** This is the retraction check on the existing build; if C3 is competitive, the audit's diagnosis was wrong and this whole redesign is misdirected.
4. **C4: every arm within noise of 0.059.** If TEST beats chance on shuffled labels, the metric leaks and nothing else in the run means anything. Run this first.
5. **μ ablation** (μ ∈ {0.5, 0.8, 1.0, 1.8}): precision@7 non-monotonic, peaking at μ < 1 in the data-poor regime. This is §6's claim that μ > 1 narrows the basin so fast pruning locks in the wrong answer. It is an independent test of the bifurcation story, and a monotone-increasing result falsifies A4 specifically without touching the rest.

### What falsifies what

- **TEST fails to beat C1 by ≥0.15 at any E ≤ 400** → the conserved flow buys nothing over counting even in the regime hand-built to favour it. **The reframing is wrong and the primitive should be retired.** This is the real null and I put it at roughly 40%.
- **TEST ≤ C2 + 0.05 everywhere** → partial null. Transitivity was the whole story; the conserved-global-signal claim specifically is dead; keep the reframing, delete A2, ship the cheap version. Perhaps 25%.
- **TEST wins but C4 also beats chance** → metric leak, discard the run.
- **μ curve monotone** → A4's bifurcation account is wrong; keep the flow, replace μ with a plain threshold.

### Cost

`pinv` of a 120×120 Laplacian ≈ 1 ms; refresh every 10 events. Largest cell: 3200 events × 20 seeds × 320 solves ≈ 2 min. Whole grid, all arms, under 15 minutes single-core. No EC2, no daemonising.

## 1. Biological/literature omissions that bear directly on "computation without a map"

**1a. Synaptic tagging and capture — the single largest omission, and it is this repo's own stated frontier.**
Frey & Morris (1997); Redondo & Morris (2011); competition for limited PRPs shown by Fonseca, Nägerl & Bonhoeffer (2004); computational forms in Clopath et al. (2008, tag–trigger–consolidation), Barrett et al. (2009), Gershman (2014, a statistical theory of STC). STC is the one biological system with *exactly* the architecture the proposal is reaching for: a **local tag** set by activity, a **scarce diffusible resource** (PRPs) spent competitively and produced *before* the outcome, and a **slow consolidation register** that determines whether the fast change persists. It also supplies, mechanistically, the thing the proposal calls "the best idea in the current build" — conjunctive credit attached to a group — via behavioural tagging / cross-tagging: a weak event becomes durable because a strong event *elsewhere* supplied the proteins. No coin, no store. The synthesis concluded "if the redesign wants learning, the immune profile is the precedent." That conclusion is wrong by omission: STC is a much closer precedent, is within-lifetime, and is in the substrate the program is nominally about. Given the repo's last commit describes the frontier as "the coherent-tag reconceptualization," this gap is conspicuous.

**1b. BCM / sliding modification threshold (Bienenstock, Cooper & Munro 1982) and metaplasticity.**
§5's rule — salience = deviation from a slowly-adapted baseline, with the slow variable setting the threshold that gates the fast update — *is* BCM's θ_M. The proposal derives it from T-cell threshold tuning and homeoviscous adaptation and never names the 1982 theory that formalised it, proved selectivity/stability results for it (Intrator & Cooper), and catalogued its failure modes. A5 is presented as "one line of code that separates a learner from a filter"; it is the BCM sliding threshold, and inherits BCM's known pathologies (runaway when θ_M adapts too slowly, collapse when too fast).

**1c. Vascular structural adaptation — Physarum's better-instrumented cousin, with a stability result that directly threatens A3.**
Pries & Secomb (1998, *AJP-Heart* 275:H349; 2001, 281:H1015). Same conductance-adaptation form, but they show **shear-only adaptation is unstable**: the flow→diameter feedback is positive, so low-flow vessels shrink toward zero and the network collapses. Stability requires an *opposing* metabolic (demand-side) stimulus plus a signal **conducted upstream along the wall**. A3 (`dD/dt = f(s) − γD`) has growth-with-flux and uniform decay and no opposing demand term. The proposal's own §4 qualification 1 gestures at scarcity gating but does not supply the negative feedback that this literature says is required. This is the most concrete predicted failure mode missing from the document.

**1d. Adaptive flow networks / leaf venation — this literature already answers the proposal's self-declared "single most attackable assumption."**
Hu & Cai, *PRL* 111:138701 (2013) prove the local adaptation dynamics performs **gradient descent on a global energy functional**, and identify a **phase transition in the source/sink statistics**: strongly fluctuating sinks → hierarchical loops, static sinks → trees. Katifori, Szöllősi & Magnasco (*PRL* 2010) and Corson (*PRL* 2010) show the same. Consequence for A2: "all-pairs within the active set" is not an epistemically neutral modelling choice — it is a *fixed, non-fluctuating* source ensemble, which puts the dynamics on the tree side of a known phase boundary. The proposal flags this as an unresolved worry (Uncertainty 1) when the literature says it is a controlled variable with a known phase diagram. It also supplies the convergence guarantee the proposal says it lacks.

**1e. Ant recruitment / stigmergy (Goss, Aron, Deneubourg & Pasteels 1989; Deneubourg et al. 1990).**
The other canonical map-free structure-former, and it is a **counterexample to the synthesis's load-bearing claim I3/§6** that "(b) is licensed exactly where a conserved global signal exists." Pheromone is *not* conserved — it is produced and evaporates — and the colony still selects the short path by autocatalysis against decay. If that holds, the Laplacian solve (A2) is not required to license over-connect-then-prune, and the cheap non-flow version is not the concession the proposal treats it as.

**1f. Non-neural within-lifetime learning — D7's "honest count: one" is undercounted.**
*Physarum* habituates and spontaneously recovers (Boisseau, Vogel & Dussutour, *Proc R Soc B* 2016). *Stentor coeruleus* habituates at the single-cell level (Rajan, Ramkumar & Marshall, *Curr Biol* 2023; Dexter, Prabakaran & Gunawardena, *Curr Biol* 2019). Eckert, Levin, Kuchling et al. (*Curr Biol* 2024) give biochemically plausible habituation models with the *same* fast/slow structure the proposal wants. Since "not one of these systems learns within a lifetime" is the premise for recommending the immune profile, this matters.

**1g. Successor representation / predictive map** (Dayan 1993; Stachenfeld, Botvinick & Gershman, *Nat Neurosci* 2017). An online, reward-free, stream-fed structure learned by TD whose **eigenvectors are the carving** — i.e. a biologically grounded algorithm that already does what the proposal wants, and whose spectral readout is the well-behaved alternative to A6. Missing from both documents.

**1h. Also absent and cheap to add:** activity-dependent synaptic pruning under a conserved trophic budget (Purves' neurotrophic hypothesis; retinal-wave-driven refinement) — the neural instance of exactly (b); Turrigiano's synaptic scaling as the homeostatic counter-arm; and fungal mycelial networks (Fricker, Boddy, Heaton), which are Physarum-like but **growing**, answering A3's admitted `f(0)=0` monotone-subtractive defect.

**1i. A contradiction to resolve:** the brief states the sweep covered climbing plants; the synthesis states no vine finding was in the input and the proposal repeats this (Uncertainty 6). One of these is wrong. The relevant literature (Ceccarini et al., *Sci Rep* 2020, on *Pisum sativum* tendril kinematics; Guerra et al.) shows prospective, movement-based search for an unseen support — a genuine no-map case — and neither document uses it.

## 2. The framework that already formalises this schema

**It is Markov Clustering (MCL), van Dongen 2000 — and the proposal's TEST arm and its C2 control are MCL's two operators.**
MCL alternates **expansion** (matrix power = C2's `N@N` transitive smoothing) with **inflation** (elementwise power then renormalise = A4's μ dial, which in MCL is *the* granularity/resolution parameter), iterated to convergence, output = a partition, k not supplied. The proposal's "conditional growth with saturating exponent μ against decay, run to relaxation, read the carving" is MCL with a resistance-based rather than random-walk propagation operator and an online rather than batch update. **Honest verdict: yes, the proposal is close to a special case of MCL**, and its own C2 control is half of MCL — which is why C2 is the control it correctly fears.

Adjacent formalisations it must also position against:
- **Effective-resistance / commute-time embedding**: Klein & Randić (1993); Fouss et al. (*IEEE TKDE* 2007). A6 is textbook commute-time similarity.
- **Physarum solver ≡ mirror descent / interior-point for min-cost flow**: Straszak & Vishnoi; Bonifaci, Mehlhorn & Varma (2012) prove convergence to shortest paths. A2+A3 is a known algorithm with known guarantees, not a new primitive.
- **Flow-based community detection with a resolution knob**: Infomap / the map equation (Rosvall & Bergstrom, *PNAS* 2008); Markov stability (Delvenne, Yaliraki & Barahona, *PNAS* 2010); diffusion maps (Coifman & Lafon 2006).
- **BCM** (§5) and **ART** (Grossberg — vigilance parameter: "is this above background? if yes commit, else reset") for the salience/commitment schema.
- **The discovery of structural form**, Kemp & Tenenbaum, *PNAS* 105:10687 (2008) — the canonical formalisation of discovering the carving *and its form* from data, over graph grammars. If the claim is structure discovery, this is the framework the claim must be stated against.
- **(Hyper)graph stochastic block models**: the §6 generator *is* a sparse hypergraph SBM with distractor noise. That is a solved estimation problem with published thresholds.

**Critically missing risk: A6's readout has a theorem against it.** von Luxburg, Radl & Hein show that in large graphs the commute/resistance distance **converges to `1/d_i + 1/d_j`** — it stops encoding global structure entirely ("Getting lost in space", NIPS 2010; *JMLR* 15:1751–1798, 2014). At n=120 with a dense learned `D`, `-R_eff` may be measuring nothing but degree. This belongs in the pre-registration as a mandatory control: score the trivial predictor `-(1/d_i + 1/d_j)` as an arm. If it matches TEST, A6 is degenerate and the flow solve is unread.

## 1. BIOLOGICAL OVERREACH

**1.1 — "Conserved global signal" is asserted for a quantity that is provably not conserved. This is fatal to A2, the one load-bearing addition.**

The licence the proposal invokes is Thomson's principle: the flow Q minimises Σ R_e Q_e² and therefore each edge's flux encodes the whole topology. That theorem holds for **one flow, with one source and one sink, on fixed conductances**. What A2 actually feeds into the update is:

```
Q_ij = sum over pairs (s,u) in A_t of |Q_ij(s,u)|
```

The absolute value destroys Kirchhoff's node law, and the sum over pairs destroys the variational characterisation. `Σ|Q(s,u)|` is a positive edge measure, not a flow: it satisfies no conservation law at any node, and it is not the minimiser of any energy. It is not a solution to a globally constrained problem; it is a *statistic of* a family of such solutions.

## 4. REINVENTION

**4.1 — A2 is current-flow betweenness centrality.** `Σ over source-sink pairs of |Q_ij(s,u)|` is the textbook definition (Newman 2005; Brandes & Fleischer 2005), restricted to sources in A_t. It is a known network-science measure with a known, documented pathology: strong degree/hub bias. The proposal names hub-swallowing as "the failure mode I would otherwise expect first" and patches it with a z-score on the *update*, while the *readout* (A6, R_eff) is untouched — and the readout is where the bias actually bites.

**4.2 — A6 is resistance-distance / commute-time clustering** (Fouss et al. 2007), and it has a specific, citable death sentence in exactly this regime. von Luxburg, Radl & Hein (2010/2014, *Getting lost in space*) prove that on large graphs the resistance distance degenerates to `R_eff(i,j) → 1/d_i + 1/d_j` — a function of the two degrees alone, carrying **no global information whatsoever**. The redesign's D is a *complete* graph (see 4.6) with a sparse strong-edge overlay, which is the most favourable possible setting for that degeneracy. Add this as a mandatory arm: **rank by `1/d_i + 1/d_j`**. If it tracks the flow arm, A2 and A6 are both dead and you have measured degrees with extra steps.

**4.3 — §5's salience rule is BCM (Bienenstock–Cooper–Munro, 1982).** A sliding modification threshold set by a slow running average of activity, gating a Hebbian growth term against decay, is the canonical 44-year-old model. The proposal derives it from T-cell threshold tuning and homeoviscous adaptation and never names it. For a document whose entire selling point is "derived from biology rather than reinvented," missing the most famous biologically-derived adaptive-threshold plasticity rule in neuroscience is not a small oversight. The z-score-by-running-variance is additionally the normalisation used in RND-style novelty bonuses.

**4.4 — A3 is competitive Hebbian learning with edge ageing, i.e. Growing Neural Gas / topology-representing networks** (Martinetz & Schulten 1994; Fritzke 1995). Edge created/strengthened on co-activation, unconditional ageing, deletion at threshold — this *is* I5's "conditional growth against unconditional decay," discovered in 1994, reward-free, and with a convergence story. The proposal's I5 is presented as a biological invariant recovered from six physics systems.

**4.5 — A4's μ is ART's vigilance parameter.** A single scalar setting how readily existing structure absorbs new input versus fragmenting. Same role, same trade-off, same failure mode (too tight → over-fragmentation locks in early errors).

**4.6 — And a hard bug in A2/A3 that the reinvention would have caught.** `Q_ij = D_ij * (Lp[i,s] − ...)`. If `D_ij = 0`, then `Q_ij = 0` exactly, so `s_ij = 0`, so `f(s_ij) = 0` (the proposal notes `f(0)=0`), so `dD_ij/dt = 0`. **D ≡ 0 is an absorbing fixed point, and every zero edge is individually absorbing.** Growth is multiplicative in D: an edge that is not already there can never appear. Therefore D must be initialised to a positive `D_floor` on **all** C(120,2) = 7140 pairs — a complete graph — and `D_floor` becomes a critical, unmentioned hyperparameter that sets the background conductance against which the whole readout must compete. With uniform background c and n = 120, a strengthened edge only shifts R_eff once its conductance exceeds roughly n·c/2. Set D_floor too high and the readout is uninformative while everything "runs"; set it too low and pinv is numerically dominated by the near-disconnected structure. That is exactly the cost R6 condemns in the random `positions` — *"a wrong basis fails silently"* — reappearing as the initialisation the proposal never specifies. Deleting the geometric prior and replacing it with an unstated uniform complete-graph prior is not removing an imported basis; it is swapping one for another and not writing it down.

**4.7 — Two more unspecified-and-outcome-determining items.** (i) `pinv` of a disconnected Laplacian returns finite cross-component values that are block-centering artefacts, not distances; the decoder ranks over all 119 others including disconnected ones, so the readout silently produces garbage *precisely when the carving has succeeded*. Define cross-component R_eff as +∞ and say so before the run. (ii) A5's slow register: `T_slow >> tau = 216`, and the data-poor budgets are E ∈ {100, 200, 400}. `Qbar` and `Qvar` will have seen **at most two time constants, and as little as half of one**, over the entire run. The variable declared to be "one line of code and what separates a learner from a filter" is guaranteed to spend the whole of the regime where a win is predicted inside its initialisation transient. §5's timescale requirement and §6's event budget are mutually incompatible as written.

2. **Cosine / common-neighbours on N.** One line, zero parameters. Given the generator has *zero cross-group edges*, this is the obvious method and it is conspicuously absent from an arm set that includes raw counts and N + λN².
3. **Spectral embedding of N** (top-8 eigenvectors of the normalised Laplacian, k-means), the textbook answer to planted-partition recovery. If it beats TEST, the "conserved global signal" bought less than an eigendecomposition.
4. **Degree-only null: rank by `1/d_i + 1/d_j`.** Tests 4.2 directly.
5. **Ablate the flow, keep the rule:** set `s_ij` = co-activation indicator. This is plain Hebbian growth-versus-decay and is the *only* clean isolation of A2's contribution.
6. **Ablate A5:** set `s = Q` with no z-score. If unchanged, the thing declared to separate a learner from a filter is dead weight — and per 4.7(ii) it is in transient anyway.
7. **Honest C3** per 3.3: co-membership frequency from `components()`, no reward.
8. State that the event stream is byte-identical across arms per seed, not merely that the seed integer matches. "Paired across 20 seeds" is ambiguous and the pairing is what the Wilcoxon rests on.

Also: 6 budgets × 5+ arms × 4 μ values with p < 0.01 and no multiple-comparison correction. With 20 paired seeds the Wilcoxon floor is p ≈ 2e-6, so p < 0.01 is trivially attainable for a negligible effect; the effect-size thresholds are doing all the work, and per 3.2 they are arbitrary and unachievable at one of the three budgets.
