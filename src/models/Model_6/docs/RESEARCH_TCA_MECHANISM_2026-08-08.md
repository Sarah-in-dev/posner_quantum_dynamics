# The MECHANISM of dopamine-mediated temporal credit assignment — grounding for the Model-6 thesis

**2026-08-08. Cited research pass (primary sources), framed on the KNOWN/UNKNOWN boundary per Sarah's
point: temporal credit assignment is an established phenomenon whose biological MECHANISM is not
understood.** Two load-bearing anchors independently re-verified at source by the physics thread:
Yagishita 2014 (dopamine window 0.3–2 s, PubMed 25258080) and **Shindou 2019 (striatal eligibility trace
~2 s, Ca-permeable AMPARs, authors state "other mechanisms must be invoked to explain the timecourse" —
PMC6585681, VERIFIED)**.

## Bottom line
The DOWNSTREAM READOUT is understood: a dopamine transient, via D1/D2 → cAMP/PKA/DARPP-32 → PP-1 →
AMPA/NMDA, converts an already-primed synapse into lasting LTP/LTD, and (Yagishita 2014) does so only in a
0.3–2 s retroactive window set by dendritic PDE. **The UPSTREAM HALF — the eligibility trace, the physical
mark that says "this synapse was just active, hold it for a verdict" — has NO established molecular
identity, and every measured trace is far too short for the seconds-to-minutes gap behavior requires.**

## The KNOWN/UNKNOWN ledger (the useful part)
**ESTABLISHED:** phasic DA ≈ RPE (Schultz/Dayan/Montague 1997); corticostriatal plasticity is
DA/D1-dependent (Reynolds et al. 2001; Calabresi 2000); the readout cascade D1(Gs)/D2(Gi)→cAMP→PKA→
DARPP-32→PP-1 (Greengard 1999); the 0.3–2 s retroactive DA window with cAMP/PKA/PDE (Yagishita 2014); a
"silent" trace can be retroactively converted by later DA (Brzosko 2015; Shindou 2019); traces are
distinct for LTP vs LTD and neuromodulator-specific (He 2015 — NE/5-HT, NOT DA, in cortex); behavioral-
timescale (~seconds) plasticity exists (BTSP, Bittner & Magee 2017); DA spreads ~µm in ~ms, so specificity
comes from the LOCAL trace not DA's spatial pattern (Liu/Goel/Kaeser 2021); STC tag decays <3 h (Frey &
Morris 1997).

**UNKNOWN / OPEN (where a candidate mechanism lives):**
1. **The molecular identity of the eligibility trace** — NO consensus. Ca-permeable AMPARs (Shindou, self-
   declared incomplete), CaMKII autophosphorylation (analogy only), STC tag (uncharacterized — "no direct
   observation of a tag-mediated capture has been reported", PMC6673289), a cAMP/PKA compartment
   (Yagishita) — none established.
2. **The true lifetime of the trace**, and whether the ~0.3–5 s electrophysiological trace and the ~1–3 h
   STC tag are even the same object.
3. **How the temporal gap to minute-scale behavior is bridged at a single synapse** — UNSOLVED. Measured
   traces 0.3–5 s; behavior spans seconds-to-minutes. TD-chaining "sidesteps rather than solves" it (bridges
   at the circuit level by NEVER requiring a long molecular trace). Ramps/WM/replay/STC unproven for this.
   **No experiment shows a single identified synapse holding a mark across a naturalistic delay.**
4. The true spatial GRAIN of credit (spine vs branch vs neuron).
5. Berke's problem — how a target neuron tells "learn" from "move" for the same DA transient.
6. Whether high-DA→LTP / low-DA→LTD is a real law or a convenient summary.
7. The whole loop is UNCLOSED: no end-to-end causal demo of a trace set → held across delay → read out by
   DA → behaviorally-correct weight change at identified synapses.

## The level-of-description gap (the through-line)
RPE/distributional theories (Schultz; Dabney 2020) say WHAT is computed, not how credit lands on synapses.
Three-factor rules (Reynolds & Wickens 2002; Frémaux & Gerstner 2015; Gerstner 2018) say HOW it should map
but POSIT an eligibility trace they cannot name molecularly. The cascade work (Yagishita; Shindou; Brzosko)
shows the readout but not the held mark. No framework spans representation → held trace → synapse-specific
change end-to-end.

## POSITIONING THE MODEL-6 THESIS AGAINST THIS (the point of the exercise)
**Our model is a candidate answer to the single biggest unknown — the eligibility trace's identity AND
lifetime — and its distinctive contribution is the exact property the field's hardest open problem needs:**
- **Proposal:** the eligibility trace = the coherent ³¹P nuclear-spin state in Ca-phosphate (Posner) dimers;
  lifetime ~100 s (the coherence window). A physical, synapse-local mark (specificity constraint met — the
  trace selects, DA gates), long enough to bridge the seconds-to-minutes gap that biology CANNOT explain at
  a single synapse (unknown #3). This is precisely the hole in the ledger.
- **The correction to my earlier error:** the established DA readout window (0.3–2 s, PDE-limited, Yagishita)
  belongs to biology's CLASSICAL short trace. It is NOT our trace's lifetime. Imposing 0.3–2 s on the quantum
  trace (as F3 Phase A/B did) defeats the thesis. The two are DIFFERENT objects — a classical ~2 s trace
  (cAMP/PKA/PDE) and a proposed quantum ~100 s trace (Posner coherence) — and can coexist.
- **What our readout must be (the open build):** dopamine reads out the LONG coherent trace — Sarah's
  "dopamine-triggered decoherence": DA collapses the coherent tag, at whatever delay it arrives, within the
  coherence lifetime. This is a NEW readout proposal for a NEW (quantum) trace; it must be shown to COEXIST
  with (not contradict) the established D1/cAMP/PKA cascade, not replace it.
- **What our model must still explain / not violate (constraints, from the report's closing):** sign-correct
  plasticity (burst→LTP / dip→LTD; D1-direct/D2-indirect); local specificity (met — the trace); DA's µm/ms
  spatial physics (met — trace selects, DA gates); pre/post coincidence prerequisite (met — activity forms
  dimers); tag/expression dissociability; the learn-vs-move disambiguation (Berke — open for us too).
