## 3. THE ADVISOR REVIEW + SHARPENED OPEN QUESTIONS (this is where to look for next moves)

All in `PO10_ADVISOR_UPDATE_UNIT_C_2026-07-22.md` §7 + both addenda. The live questions:

1. **Is 0.75 the honest ceiling, or removable readout noise?** The advisor's read: a perfect partition readout
   should decode at ceiling, so 0.75 is noise. But the naive fix (magnitude/rectified sign) **leaks** (§1). So
   the real question: **can a *covariance-across-trials* readout recover domain co-membership from a rectified
   signal WITHOUT the abundance leak?** (Co-membered clusters co-vary in magnitude trial-to-trial; abundance
   does not.) This is the crux and a genuine build, not a flag-flip.
2. **The sign / where reward enters (Q2).** Advisor's proposal: the partition carries *which* synapses share
   fate; the neuromodulator carries *what* the fate is. Dopamine/CaMKII **rectifies** the collapse (LTP/LTD not
   mirror-image) → directional Δw, sign-noise leaves the channel. BUT §1 shows naive rectification reopens the
   marginal confound — so this must be paired with a confound-immune readout (Q1). Honest cost: multi-trial
   consolidation despite one-shot binding (= BTSP).
3. **Fragmentation (advisor Loss-2).** Measure the fraction of each cluster's dimers in its modal domain. If
   well below 1, that within-cluster fragmentation dilutes the shared-sign signal. **Harness already
   instrumented:** every record now has `cluster_frag` (per-cluster modal-domain fraction) — just re-run and read it.
4. **Compressed inter-pair interval (advisor Loss-1).** Counterbalancing cost 2× signal (each pair only readable
   when late). The real fix: compress the interval so both pairs are equally young at readout — the limit is the
   **η STROBE width** (ignition overlap), NOT dimer lifetime (dimers live ~200 s; what blocks A–C is
   non-overlapping ignition). ⚠ Harness does NOT yet record the η time-course — add a downsampled η trace to
   `one_run` to measure the strobe, then re-run Pilot A at the compressed interval to confirm the hard zeros survive.
5. **Capacity scaling (Q3).** Go to 6 clusters; ask how many distinct pairings are separable. Holds ⇒ high-capacity
   representation; saturates at ~1 bit ⇒ coincidence detector. This is the measurement that answers Q1 rather
   than estimates it.

**Advisor's recommended sequence:** decoder noise check (DONE — decoder unbiased, ceiling achievable) → the
covariance/fixed-sign question → fragmentation → compressed interval → 6-cluster capacity.
